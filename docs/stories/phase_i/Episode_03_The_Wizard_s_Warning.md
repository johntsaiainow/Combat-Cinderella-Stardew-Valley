# Episode 3 — The Wizard's Warning

**Sunday, December 1, 2024**  
**Theme:** Science and magic can observe the same mystery without explaining it the same way.

## Cold Open — Unreasonable Evidence

### Scene 1 — Ko Estate Workshop · 6:48 A.M.

The workshop light has been on all night. Aether Gear compares the metal fragment with the Southern California artifact. Their materials differ, but certain machining patterns and periodic signal characteristics are strikingly close.

Carrie arrives with hot cocoa.

**CARRIE:** You stayed up all night?

**AETHER GEAR:** I slept.

**CARRIE:** How long?

**AETHER GEAR:** Twenty minutes.

**CARRIE:** Mom's going to be mad.

**AETHER GEAR:** Then don't tell her.

**CARRIE:** You said that last time.

He opens a map and points to a tower in Cindersap Forest.

**AETHER GEAR:** We need a local expert.

**CARRIE:** You're actually going to visit Rasmodius?

**SAPPHIRE FOX (from the stairs):** Visit who?

**CARRIE:** A researcher.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Researching what?

**CARRIE:** Magic.

**AETHER GEAR:** She means geomagnetism.

Sapphire Fox folds her arms.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I think the three of you know more about this place than you're telling me.

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — The Farmer's Ordinary Morning

### Scene 2 — The Farm · 9:15 A.M.

Sapphire Fox and Carrie find the farmer working at impossible speed: hoeing soil, repairing fences, and retrieving a cheese press from a backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Carrie. That was a whole machine.

**CARRIE:** Yes.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why aren't you surprised?

**CARRIE:** Because he's the farmer?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That is not an answer.

The farmer agrees to guide them to the Wizard.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** What does this Wizard do for a living?

**FARMER:** Magic.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I mean his profession.

**FARMER:** Wizard.

Sapphire Fox closes her notebook.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm beginning to worry about the education system here.

### Scene 3 — Cindersap Forest · 10:40 A.M.

On the walk, the farmer gathers mushrooms, picks up a winter root, and chops a tree without breaking stride.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Aren't we on a mission?

**FARMER:** Sure.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Then why are you chopping down a tree?

**FARMER:** It's on the way.

**CARRIE (quietly):** At least he hasn't started fishing.

## Act II — The Tower

### Scene 4 — Wizard's Tower · 11:25 A.M.

The tower door opens on its own. Sapphire Fox instinctively reaches toward her jacket; Carrie gently stops her.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That wooden door opened itself.

**CARRIE:** Automatic door?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** It's made of wood!

Inside, Rasmodius welcomes the farmer and the visitors.

**WIZARD:** I sensed you would bring guests today.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Security cameras?

**WIZARD:** No. The wind tells me certain things.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** So... security cameras hidden in the trees?

**WIZARD:** Magic.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** There it is again.

Aether Gear and Natalica arrive with the sealed fragment. The Wizard's expression changes when he sees it.

**WIZARD:** Where did you find this?

**AETHER GEAR:** In the mines. Our friend found it.

The candles tremble as the Wizard holds his hand above the metal.

**WIZARD:** This object is not of the valley's magic. But something like it has been here for a very long time.

**AETHER GEAR:** How long?

**WIZARD:** Longer than your arrival here.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Could you possibly be more specific?

**WIZARD:** Some answers must be discovered.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I hate that kind of answer.

## Act III — Two Ways of Measuring the Unknown

### Scene 5 — Wizard's Tower Laboratory · 1:10 P.M.

Aether Gear places a sensor near the fragment. Rasmodius arranges rune stones around it.

**AETHER GEAR:** I can measure a weak periodic signal. Can you repeat whatever you just did?

The Wizard raises his hand. Violet light shimmers. The sensor waveform changes.

**AETHER GEAR:** Again, please.

The effect repeats.

**AETHER GEAR:** Excellent. Reproducible results!

**NATALICA (to Carrie):** Dad is running experiments on a wizard.

**CARRIE:** Of course he is.

Sapphire Fox watches the farmer retrieve a pumpkin pie and then an entire platter of cheese from the backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** How much food do you have in there?

**FARMER:** Some more.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Don't tell me.

The Wizard suggests another witness.

**WIZARD:** Speak with the one who lives beneath the town.

**NATALICA:** Krobus!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The one who lives in the sewer?

**CARRIE:** He's very nice.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** How do you know that?!

## Act IV — Into the Mines

### Scene 6 — The Mines · 3:35 P.M.

Sapphire Fox insists on accompanying the farmer into the mines. She brings a flashlight, first-aid supplies, and communications equipment. The farmer brings a sword and a hat.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No helmet?

**FARMER:** I have a hat.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Not the same thing.

A green slime springs from the shadows.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Unknown organism! Keep your distance!

The farmer dispatches it with a casual swing.

**FARMER:** Slime.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Those are just... here?

**FARMER:** Pretty much.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Does the town council know?

**FARMER:** Probably.

Further in, they find fresh, regular grooves in the stone near a narrow crack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** These aren't natural fractures. Something mechanical cut this wall.

**FARMER:** This wasn't here last week.

Sapphire Fox photographs the marks and calls Aether Gear.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** We have recent excavation evidence. Something has been working in the lower tunnels, and it isn't a normal mining crew.

## Act V — The Right Question

### Scene 7 — Ko Estate · 7:20 P.M.

The family reviews the photographs. The grooves resemble marks on the Southern California artifact, though the match is not yet conclusive.

**AETHER GEAR:** Someone may have been working here before us.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** And we still don't know what they want.

**NATALICA:** Can I go into the mines tomorrow?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No.

**NATALICA:** With the farmer?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Still no.

**EPILOGUE — THE SEWERS · 10:16 P.M.**

Krobus examines a crack in the sewer wall. Silver light leaks through. Three heavy knocks echo beneath the town.

**KROBUS:** It's happening again.

Far below, a tiny red light appears in the darkness—and vanishes.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
