# Episode 4 — The Shadow in the Sewers

**Monday, December 2, 2024**  
**Theme:** The machines aren't attacking the valley. They are searching beneath it.

## Cold Open — A Visitor at Midnight

### Scene 1 — Pelican Town Sewers · 2:17 A.M.

Krobus stands before a metal door that does not belong in the ancient drainage tunnels. Fresh drill holes surround the frame.

A red indicator flashes.

**MACHINE:** SUBSURFACE SURVEY. SECTOR FOUR.

**KROBUS:** Oh, no. Not again.

**MACHINE:** BIOLOGICAL PRESENCE DETECTED.

Krobus jumps back.

**KROBUS:** I live here!

**MACHINE:** NON-TARGET ORGANISM.

The light goes out.

**KROBUS:** How rude.

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — Monday at Ko Estate

### Scene 2 — Breakfast · 8:05 A.M.

Natalica studies the farmer's hand-drawn map. Carrie drinks cocoa. Aether Gear checks signal logs over breakfast.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Who's doing the dishes?

**NATALICA:** Carrie!

**CARRIE:** I did them yesterday!

**NATALICA:** Then you're experienced!

**AETHER GEAR:** Last person to finish washes up.

Natalica immediately stuffs the last piece of toast into her mouth.

**CARRIE:** Cheater.

Aether Gear announces the day's destination.

**AETHER GEAR:** We're visiting Krobus.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The person in the sewer?

**NATALICA:** He's not a person. He's a shadow person.

A long silence.

**AETHER GEAR:** He's a member of a local minority community.

**CARRIE:** Dad!

**AETHER GEAR:** Technically true.

### Scene 3 — The River · 10:20 A.M.

The farmer is fishing. After greeting the family, the farmer packs away a rod, a crab pot, a fish, and a recycling machine in the same backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm not asking anymore.

**FARMER:** Ready?

**SAPPHIRE FOX (to Carrie):** Your father calls this person an ordinary farmer. Their daily workload would exhaust an engineering battalion.

**CARRIE:** They're very efficient.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm starting to suspect they don't sleep.

**CARRIE:** Not after two in the morning, anyway.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** What was that?

**CARRIE:** Nothing.

## Act II — A Friend Underground

### Scene 4 — Sewer Entrance · 11:06 A.M.

The farmer produces a rusty key.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Where did you get a sewer key?

**FARMER:** Gunther gave it to me.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The museum curator?

**FARMER:** I donated a lot of things.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Public infrastructure works differently here.

### Scene 5 — Krobus's Shop

Krobus welcomes the farmer warmly, then shrinks back at the sight of the strangers. The farmer introduces them as friends investigating unusual machinery.

When Aether Gear opens the sample case, Krobus recoils.

**KROBUS:** Don't bring that near me!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why?

**KROBUS:** It wakes them up. The walking iron things. They've been digging below us.

**AETHER GEAR:** Robots?

**KROBUS:** They move without being alive. And they're not Dwarf machines.

He points down a narrow corridor.

**KROBUS:** They are digging for something.

## Act III — The Door Beneath the Town

### Scene 6 — Sealed Passage · 12:35 P.M.

Aether Gear studies the alloy door. Sapphire Fox kneels beside fresh tread marks.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Small tracked vehicle. Tight turning radius. Probably unmanned.

**AETHER GEAR:** A survey platform.

Carrie spots the same three-slash emblem on the frame.

**CARRIE:** That symbol looks familiar.

**AETHER GEAR:** Similar to the one on our California artifact.

**NATALICA:** So it's the same people?

**AETHER GEAR:** Related, perhaps. We can't prove that yet.

Sapphire Fox studies the excavation.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You're all asking who built these machines. I want to know what they're looking for.

A red light suddenly scans the group.

**MACHINE:** UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS DETECTED.

A half-height tracked survey robot rolls through the door. Its camera and manipulator arm swivel toward Aether Gear's sample case.

**MACHINE:** RECOVERABLE COMPONENT DETECTED. RETURN PROPERTY TO SURVEY SYSTEM.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Stop.

It continues forward. Sapphire Fox jams a metal bar into the tread. The farmer strikes the outer housing with a pickaxe. Aether Gear identifies and disconnects an exposed power connector.

**MACHINE:** MOBILITY ERROR.

The robot goes still.

**FARMER:** Think it drops loot?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You're thinking about loot *now*?

## Act IV — A Search, Not an Invasion

### Scene 7 — Ko Estate Workshop · 4:50 P.M.

Aether Gear dismantles the recovered survey robot. Its controller has an unusual mixture of old and newer components.

**AETHER GEAR:** No manufacturer label. No ordinary commercial markings.

Natalica points at a board.

**NATALICA:** Is that an ESP32?

**AETHER GEAR:** No. Similar-looking package, though.

A plaque reads **SUBSURFACE RESOURCE SURVEY**.

**CARRIE:** So they're mining?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Maybe. But a survey network is not the same as a mine.

She overlays the mine, sewer, and mountain anomaly locations on a map.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Someone built a system to investigate a specific region underground.

**AETHER GEAR:** And its control center should still be somewhere in the mountains.

**FARMER:** I know a route near there.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Of course you do.

### In-Game Quest Concept — Quest 03: The Hidden Survey

> **Objectives:** Inspect three suspicious mountain locations; photograph excavation marks; avoid machinery; report to Ko Estate.  
> **Reward:** 3,000g and increased Ko family friendship.  
> **Status:** Story concept; not implemented in v0.8.6-beta.

## Epilogue — The System Notices

### Scene 8 — Northern Mountains · 11:48 P.M.

An underground terminal flashes:

```text
SURVEY UNIT 04 — OFFLINE
UNAUTHORIZED HUMAN INTERFERENCE — CONFIRMED
EXTERNAL SIGNATURE DETECTED
MATCH FOUND: SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA INCIDENT
RECLASSIFY LOCAL ACTIVITY: PRIORITY HIGH
```

A larger machine wakes in the dark.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
