# Episode 5 — The Forgotten Machine

**Tuesday, December 3, 2024**  
**Theme:** An old machine may be doing a job no one remembers assigning it.

## Cold Open — Station 07

### Scene 1 — Underground Survey Station · 3:16 A.M.

A large machine starts inside an abandoned tunnel. An old terminal flickers green.

```text
AUTONOMOUS SURVEY NETWORK — STATION 07
SURVEY UNITS ONLINE: 3 / 8
COMMUNICATION LINK: DEGRADED
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: LOCATE SUBSURFACE ANOMALY
```

A tracked unit places a rock sample into a scanner.

```text
SAMPLE REJECTED
TARGET SIGNATURE NOT FOUND
CONTINUE SEARCH
```

Another alert appears.

```text
UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS: SECTOR 04
MISSING SURVEY COMPONENT
EXTERNAL INTERFERENCE: HUMAN
ACTIVATE SECURITY PROTOCOL
```

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — Old Technology, New Repairs

### Scene 2 — Ko Estate Workshop · 9:10 A.M.

Aether Gear examines the robot's circuit boards. Carrie records component numbers. Natalica studies the sensor assembly.

**NATALICA:** Is that LiDAR?

**AETHER GEAR:** Something similar. But the scan pattern is unusual.

**CARRIE:** Can we identify the manufacturer?

**AETHER GEAR:** Not yet. Some of this design is decades old. Other parts are much newer.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** So the machine has been repaired and upgraded over time.

She displays photographs of two different tread patterns.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The smaller tracks belong to our survey robot. The larger ones suggest a heavier transport or construction platform.

**AETHER GEAR:** Then there's more equipment underground.

## Act II — Three Locations

### Scene 3 — Northern Mines · 10:25 A.M.

The farmer reaches the first survey point and photographs regular cuts in the rock. Behind a loose stone sits a powered sensor. Remembering Aether Gear's warning, the farmer leaves it in place.

**FARMER:** For once, I'll listen.

### Scene 4 — Old Mountain Trail · 12:40 P.M.

At the second point, the farmer finds a metal box marked with three diagonal lines.

```text
REMOTE NODE 12
STATUS: ACTIVE
```

The farmer photographs it and continues.

### Scene 5 — Abandoned Drainage Outlet · 2:15 P.M.

The third location conceals a dry concrete tunnel lined with power cables and fresh tread marks. A larger survey robot emerges from the darkness.

**MACHINE:** UNAUTHORIZED PERSONNEL DETECTED. LEAVE RESTRICTED AREA.

**FARMER:** I'm just taking pictures.

**MACHINE:** LEAVE RESTRICTED AREA.

The farmer thinks for a moment.

**FARMER:** Okay.

The farmer walks away. The robot, apparently satisfied, retreats.

## Act III — The Shape of the Network

### Scene 6 — Ko Estate · 4:30 P.M.

Aether Gear places the three locations on a map. They form a rough perimeter around an isolated section of mountain.

**AETHER GEAR:** These aren't random installations.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** They're monitoring something in the center.

**CARRIE:** What's there?

**FARMER:** An old road to a sealed mine.

**AETHER GEAR:** Then that's where the control center may be.

Sapphire Fox studies the third-location video.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The robot warned our friend away but didn't attack. That's not ordinary hostile behavior. It's protecting an operation—or keeping people away from one.

**NATALICA:** Maybe it's guarding treasure!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** If someone has maintained these machines for decades, whatever they're after is more important than a treasure chest.

## Act IV — The Forgotten Station

### Scene 7 — Sealed Mine · 6:10 P.M.

The team visits the old mine before dark to verify the entrance. Natalica stays at Ko Estate on remote monitoring duty, to her considerable annoyance.

The farmer clears vines from a steel door. A plaque reads:

```text
STATION 07
COMMISSIONED: 1987
LAST MANUAL INSPECTION: 1994
```

**CARRIE:** Nineteen eighty-seven?

**AETHER GEAR:** Much older than we expected.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** And someone has kept it running ever since.

The terminal wakes.

```text
EXTERNAL PRESENCE DETECTED
IDENTITY VERIFICATION IN PROGRESS
SIGNATURE MATCH FOUND
SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA INCIDENT
RELATED PERSONNEL DETECTED
```

**CARRIE:** It recognizes us?

Aether Gear grips the sealed sample case.

```text
RECOVERY PROTOCOL ACTIVE
RETURN OF MISSING COMPONENT REQUESTED
```

**MACHINE:** THE COMPONENT IS REQUIRED TO COMPLETE THE SURVEY.

**AETHER GEAR:** What are you searching for?

```text
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE:
LOCATE AND CONTAIN
SUBSURFACE ANOMALY
```

**CARRIE:** Contain?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** We've been assuming the machines are hunting for a resource. What if they're trying to keep something from escaping?

## Act V — Containment Failure

### Scene 8 — Station 07 Entrance · 7:02 P.M.

The terminal screams an alarm.

```text
CONTAINMENT STATUS: CRITICAL
SUBSURFACE PRESSURE INCREASING
AUTONOMOUS SYSTEM CAPACITY: 18%
EMERGENCY RESPONSE REQUIRED
```

Heavy impacts shake the door. Pebbles fall from the rock above.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Everyone back! Now!

The farmer pulls Carrie away from the entrance. Aether Gear secures his equipment and follows.

**AETHER GEAR:** Don't shut it down! Whatever it is, the system is holding it in!

A final impact rattles the mountainside. Then silence.

### Scene 9 — Ko Estate · 10:35 P.M.

The family studies the warning logs. Sapphire Fox points to the second survey location.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** We don't have to enter the station. Restore the external control node and let the containment network coordinate itself.

**AETHER GEAR:** Remote Node 12. If we can reconnect it, the station may recover.

**NATALICA:** Can I come this time?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You can help with the monitors.

**NATALICA:** Again?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Again.

**EPILOGUE — STATION 07 · 11:59 P.M.**

```text
CONTAINMENT STATUS: CRITICAL
EXTERNAL CONTROL NODE 12: AWAITING RECONNECTION
MANUAL RECOVERY POSSIBLE

WARNING:
CONTAINED ENTITY IS NOT THE PRIMARY THREAT
```

A voice whispers in the dark:

**UNKNOWN VOICE:** THE SIGNAL HAS BEEN RECEIVED. THEY KNOW WHERE WE ARE.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
