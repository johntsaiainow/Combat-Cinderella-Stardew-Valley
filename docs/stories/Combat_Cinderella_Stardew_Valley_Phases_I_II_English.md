# Combat Cinderella — Stardew Valley Adventures

**Phase I: The Stardew Valley Incident (complete six-episode screenplay)**  
**Phase II: The Ginger Island Adventure (planned six-episode follow-up)**

**Season 1 · Two-Phase Crossover Story Collection**  
**Language:** English  
**Setting:** Stardew Valley, Washington State · November 27–December 5, 2024 (Phase I); Ginger Island · Summer 2025 (Phase II, tentative)  
**Format:** Phase I screenplay, Phase II series outline, and optional Stardew Valley mod quest concepts

> **Continuity note:** In the Combat Cinderella universe, Stardew Valley is a real place, not a video game dimension. Aether Gear introduced the *Stardew Valley* game to Carrie and Natalica before the family visited the actual valley. Sapphire Fox has never played it. The farmer is the playable protagonist of *Stardew Valley* and an essential partner in this story; the Ko family are the central characters of *Combat Cinderella*.
>
> **Production note:** Quest cards and rewards in this screenplay are narrative designs for a future mod campaign, **not claims of implementation** in the existing v0.8.6-beta content pack. Episodes 1 and 2 have been reconstructed in English from their established story beats; Episodes 3–6 adapt the completed screenplay arc.

## Cast

- **Carrie Ko / Combat Cinderella:** The older daughter, responsible and observant, with an adventurous streak.
- **Natalica Ko / Shadow Cub:** The younger daughter, enthusiastic, fearless, and eager to explore every corner of the valley.
- **Aether Gear / Mr. Ko:** Their father, an engineer who approaches mysteries through measurement, experimentation, and the occasional spectacularly questionable idea.
- **Sapphire Fox / Mrs. Ko:** Their mother, a capable former operative whose practical instincts and sharp humor keep the family grounded. She has never played *Stardew Valley*.
- **The Farmer:** The valley's extraordinarily resourceful local farmer, miner, fisher, and adventurer.
- **Marlon:** Leader of the Adventurer's Guild.
- **Rasmodius / The Wizard:** An enigmatic practitioner of the valley's magic.
- **Krobus:** A friendly shadow person living in the sewers.

# PHASE I — THE STARDEW VALLEY INCIDENT

# Episode 1 — A Strange Request

**Wednesday, November 27–Friday, November 29, 2024**  
**Theme:** A familiar place that should not be familiar.

## Cold Open — A Thanksgiving Detour

### Scene 1 — Ko Residence, Southern California · Morning

A suitcase lies open on the living-room floor. Electronics cases are stacked beside it. Aether Gear is checking a tablet while Carrie and Natalica finish breakfast.

**AETHER GEAR:** We're going to Washington for a few days.

**NATALICA:** Washington, D.C.?

**AETHER GEAR:** Washington State. There's an unusual signal I want to investigate.

**CARRIE:** During Thanksgiving?

**AETHER GEAR:** The equipment doesn't observe holidays.

Sapphire Fox enters, sees the equipment cases, and stops.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Please tell me this is a vacation.

**AETHER GEAR:** It can be both.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That's what you said last time.

Aether Gear brings up a map. A wooded valley appears on the screen.

Carrie leans forward.

**CARRIE:** Wait. Is that—?

**NATALICA:** Stardew Valley?!

**AETHER GEAR:** Pelican Town, to be precise.

The sisters stare at each other in disbelief.

**NATALICA:** The *real* Pelican Town?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why are you two acting like your father just announced Disneyland?

**CARRIE:** It's complicated.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** It's always complicated when the three of you are involved.

**CUT TO TITLE.**

## Act I — The Road to Ko Estate

### Scene 2 — Washington State · Wednesday, November 27 · Late Afternoon

The family drives through forested roads beneath a gray November sky. Their destination is Ko Estate, a secluded family property reached by a lane branching from the Backwoods.

Natalica studies the passing trees with growing excitement.

**NATALICA:** The road looks exactly right.

**CARRIE:** Even the bend near the Backwoods.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Exactly right compared with what?

**AETHER GEAR:** A map they know.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** A map from where?

Silence.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm going to regret asking, aren't I?

They arrive at Ko Estate. The house is quiet, and the air smells of wet leaves and pine. Aether Gear unloads diagnostic instruments while Sapphire Fox inspects the property and the girls race to the windows.

**CARRIE:** We really are here.

**NATALICA:** I want to see the farm!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** First you unpack. Then you can explore—with an adult.

**AETHER GEAR:** Technically, I qualify.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You are the reason I specified an adult.

## Act II — The Valley Is Real

### Scene 3 — Pelican Town · Friday, November 29 · Morning

The town square is alive with ordinary business. People walk past Pierre's store. A river runs under the bridge. Carrie and Natalica recognize places they have only known through a screen.

**NATALICA:** That's Pierre's!

**CARRIE:** And there's the route toward the Community Center.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Have you been here before?

**CARRIE:** Not exactly.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That is not a reassuring answer.

Aether Gear explains that the family is following an unusual set of signals, and that mineral samples from the local mines might help determine their source. He doesn't want the children entering hazardous tunnels.

**NATALICA:** But the mines are where the good stuff is!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That sentence is precisely why you're not going in.

### Scene 4 — The Farmer's Farm · Midday

The farmer is repairing a fence when the family arrives. Without hesitation, the farmer sets down a tool, pulls several useful items out of a backpack, and greets them.

**FARMER:** Hey there. New in town?

**AETHER GEAR:** Staying nearby for a few days. I'm investigating some unusual mineral readings.

**FARMER:** You need ore?

**AETHER GEAR:** Not ordinary ore. Samples with unusual metallic inclusions, preferably from deeper in the mines.

**FARMER:** I can look.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You go into those mines regularly?

**FARMER:** Sure. Between farming, fishing, and everything else.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Everything else?

**FARMER:** Depends on the day.

Carrie gives her father a knowing look. Natalica is barely containing her excitement.

**AETHER GEAR:** I'd rather pay someone who knows the terrain than take unnecessary risks.

**FARMER:** Fair enough.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Finally, a sensible decision.

### In-Game Quest Concept — Quest 01: A Strange Request

> **Objective:** Gather unusual mineral samples from the Mines and deliver them to Aether Gear at Ko Estate.  
> **Reward:** 1,000g.  
> **Status:** Story concept; not implemented in v0.8.6-beta.

## Act III — First Evidence

### Scene 5 — Ko Estate Workshop · Evening

The farmer returns with a collection of minerals. Aether Gear examines them beneath a microscope, comparing the readings with data from Southern California.

**AETHER GEAR:** Interesting. There's a periodic disturbance in these samples.

**CARRIE:** The same as the California signal?

**AETHER GEAR:** Similar. Not enough to call it the same source.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Then don't call it that.

**AETHER GEAR:** I wasn't going to.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You were thinking it loudly.

Natalica peers at the samples.

**NATALICA:** So tomorrow we go into the mines?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No.

**NATALICA:** I didn't even finish asking!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I recognized the direction of the question.

The farmer points to one of the darker pieces.

**FARMER:** I've seen stranger marks deeper down. And lately there's been some noise in tunnels that used to be quiet.

Aether Gear and Sapphire Fox exchange a glance.

**AETHER GEAR:** Could you show me where?

**FARMER:** I can draw a map.

The camera holds on a faint, repeating waveform on the laptop screen.

**FADE OUT.**

**NEXT: Episode 2 — Echoes Beneath the Mountain.**

# Episode 2 — Echoes Beneath the Mountain

**Saturday, November 30, 2024**  
**Theme:** Not every mystery in the valley belongs to the same world.

## Cold Open — The Engineer's Problem

### Scene 1 — Ko Estate Workshop · Early Morning

Aether Gear compares mineral readings with an artifact recovered earlier in Southern California. The waveforms share a peculiar rhythm, but their sources remain uncertain.

**AETHER GEAR:** Similar periodicity, different materials. That's correlation, not proof.

**CARRIE:** So what do we do?

**AETHER GEAR:** Get better samples.

Natalica enters carrying a sword.

**NATALICA:** I'm ready!

**AETHER GEAR:** Ready for what?

**NATALICA:** The mines.

**AETHER GEAR:** Absolutely not.

**NATALICA:** You haven't even seen my technique!

**AETHER GEAR:** That's not a persuasive argument.

**SAPPHIRE FOX (from doorway):** Whose sword is that?

Natalica quietly lowers it.

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — A Farmer With Too Many Jobs

### Scene 2 — The Farm · Late Morning

Sapphire Fox accompanies Carrie to meet the farmer. She watches the farmer harvest vegetables, feed animals, repair machinery, and prepare for a mining trip—all with improbable efficiency.

The farmer pulls a full-size chair from a backpack, sets it down, eats a lettuce leaf, and puts the chair back.

Sapphire Fox freezes.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Carrie. Did you see that?

**CARRIE:** The chair?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The *entire chair*.

**CARRIE:** Yes.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** And you think that's normal?

**CARRIE:** Normal for this farmer.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That is not an explanation.

The farmer smiles politely.

**FARMER:** Need anything before I head out?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** A detailed explanation of your backpack would be a start.

## Act II — The Adventurer's Guild

### Scene 3 — The Mountain · Afternoon

Aether Gear and Natalica visit the Adventurer's Guild. Marlon studies the map of the mines.

**MARLON:** Some of the deeper tunnels have changed. New cracks. Strange sounds. Things we didn't notice before.

**AETHER GEAR:** When did it begin?

**MARLON:** Within the past few weeks.

Aether Gear checks the dates against his signal log. Several align.

**NATALICA:** So something's digging under the mountain?

**MARLON:** Maybe. The mines have plenty of old secrets. Don't assume you know which one you're dealing with.

Aether Gear nods. It's good advice.

## Act III — Sapphire Fox Investigates

### Scene 4 — Pelican Town · Afternoon

Sapphire Fox speaks with Mayor Lewis and Dr. Harvey about the mine's history and reports of injured miners.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** People who pass out in the mines sometimes wake up missing belongings?

**HARVEY:** It has happened. My concern is their safety.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Mine too. But missing property suggests another possibility.

**LEWIS:** What possibility?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Someone may be taking advantage of them.

She writes in her notebook. Unlike Aether Gear, she isn't assuming the answer will be found in an electrical signal.

## Act IV — A Piece That Doesn't Belong

### Scene 5 — The Mines · Late Afternoon

The farmer descends into a familiar section of the mines, fights off a few creatures, and finds a newly exposed crevice. Inside lies a silvery metal fragment, unnaturally precise, marked with a small geometric symbol.

A low, rhythmic thud echoes from beyond the rock.

*THUD. THUD. THUD.*

The farmer pockets the fragment and retreats rather than chasing the sound.

### In-Game Quest Concept — Quest 02: Echoes Beneath the Mountain

> **Objective:** Collect three unusual mineral samples from the Mines and report new excavation evidence to Aether Gear.  
> **Reward:** 2,000g.  
> **Status:** Story concept; not implemented in v0.8.6-beta.

### Scene 6 — Ko Estate Workshop · Night

Aether Gear examines the fragment. Under magnification, its machining marks resemble those on the Southern California artifact.

**AETHER GEAR:** These weren't formed by geology. Someone manufactured this.

**CARRIE:** The same people?

**AETHER GEAR:** Maybe. The markings and signal behavior are close, but I still need more evidence.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** So someone was investigating this valley before we arrived.

**AETHER GEAR:** That's one possibility.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** And our farmer happened to find their equipment.

She studies the farmer carefully.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You're very comfortable in dangerous places. Have you had special training?

**FARMER:** Mostly experience.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Hm.

**FARMER:** If you want someone who knows the unusual things around here, you should talk to the Wizard.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The what?

Carrie and Natalica exchange delighted looks.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**NEXT: Episode 3 — The Wizard's Warning.**

# Episode 3 — The Wizard's Warning

**Sunday, December 1, 2024**  
**Theme:** Science and magic can observe the same mystery without explaining it the same way.

## Cold Open — Unreasonable Evidence

### Scene 1 — Ko Estate Workshop · 6:48 A.M.

The workshop light has been on all night. Aether Gear compares the metal fragment with the Southern California artifact. Their materials differ, but certain machining patterns and periodic signal characteristics are strikingly close.

Carrie arrives with hot cocoa.

**CARRIE:** You stayed up all night?

**AETHER GEAR:** I slept.

**CARRIE:** How long?

**AETHER GEAR:** Twenty minutes.

**CARRIE:** Mom's going to be mad.

**AETHER GEAR:** Then don't tell her.

**CARRIE:** You said that last time.

He opens a map and points to a tower in Cindersap Forest.

**AETHER GEAR:** We need a local expert.

**CARRIE:** You're actually going to visit Rasmodius?

**SAPPHIRE FOX (from the stairs):** Visit who?

**CARRIE:** A researcher.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Researching what?

**CARRIE:** Magic.

**AETHER GEAR:** She means geomagnetism.

Sapphire Fox folds her arms.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I think the three of you know more about this place than you're telling me.

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — The Farmer's Ordinary Morning

### Scene 2 — The Farm · 9:15 A.M.

Sapphire Fox and Carrie find the farmer working at impossible speed: hoeing soil, repairing fences, and retrieving a cheese press from a backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Carrie. That was a whole machine.

**CARRIE:** Yes.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why aren't you surprised?

**CARRIE:** Because he's the farmer?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That is not an answer.

The farmer agrees to guide them to the Wizard.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** What does this Wizard do for a living?

**FARMER:** Magic.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I mean his profession.

**FARMER:** Wizard.

Sapphire Fox closes her notebook.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm beginning to worry about the education system here.

### Scene 3 — Cindersap Forest · 10:40 A.M.

On the walk, the farmer gathers mushrooms, picks up a winter root, and chops a tree without breaking stride.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Aren't we on a mission?

**FARMER:** Sure.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Then why are you chopping down a tree?

**FARMER:** It's on the way.

**CARRIE (quietly):** At least he hasn't started fishing.

## Act II — The Tower

### Scene 4 — Wizard's Tower · 11:25 A.M.

The tower door opens on its own. Sapphire Fox instinctively reaches toward her jacket; Carrie gently stops her.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That wooden door opened itself.

**CARRIE:** Automatic door?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** It's made of wood!

Inside, Rasmodius welcomes the farmer and the visitors.

**WIZARD:** I sensed you would bring guests today.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Security cameras?

**WIZARD:** No. The wind tells me certain things.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** So... security cameras hidden in the trees?

**WIZARD:** Magic.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** There it is again.

Aether Gear and Natalica arrive with the sealed fragment. The Wizard's expression changes when he sees it.

**WIZARD:** Where did you find this?

**AETHER GEAR:** In the mines. Our friend found it.

The candles tremble as the Wizard holds his hand above the metal.

**WIZARD:** This object is not of the valley's magic. But something like it has been here for a very long time.

**AETHER GEAR:** How long?

**WIZARD:** Longer than your arrival here.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Could you possibly be more specific?

**WIZARD:** Some answers must be discovered.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I hate that kind of answer.

## Act III — Two Ways of Measuring the Unknown

### Scene 5 — Wizard's Tower Laboratory · 1:10 P.M.

Aether Gear places a sensor near the fragment. Rasmodius arranges rune stones around it.

**AETHER GEAR:** I can measure a weak periodic signal. Can you repeat whatever you just did?

The Wizard raises his hand. Violet light shimmers. The sensor waveform changes.

**AETHER GEAR:** Again, please.

The effect repeats.

**AETHER GEAR:** Excellent. Reproducible results!

**NATALICA (to Carrie):** Dad is running experiments on a wizard.

**CARRIE:** Of course he is.

Sapphire Fox watches the farmer retrieve a pumpkin pie and then an entire platter of cheese from the backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** How much food do you have in there?

**FARMER:** Some more.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Don't tell me.

The Wizard suggests another witness.

**WIZARD:** Speak with the one who lives beneath the town.

**NATALICA:** Krobus!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The one who lives in the sewer?

**CARRIE:** He's very nice.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** How do you know that?!

## Act IV — Into the Mines

### Scene 6 — The Mines · 3:35 P.M.

Sapphire Fox insists on accompanying the farmer into the mines. She brings a flashlight, first-aid supplies, and communications equipment. The farmer brings a sword and a hat.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No helmet?

**FARMER:** I have a hat.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Not the same thing.

A green slime springs from the shadows.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Unknown organism! Keep your distance!

The farmer dispatches it with a casual swing.

**FARMER:** Slime.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Those are just... here?

**FARMER:** Pretty much.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Does the town council know?

**FARMER:** Probably.

Further in, they find fresh, regular grooves in the stone near a narrow crack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** These aren't natural fractures. Something mechanical cut this wall.

**FARMER:** This wasn't here last week.

Sapphire Fox photographs the marks and calls Aether Gear.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** We have recent excavation evidence. Something has been working in the lower tunnels, and it isn't a normal mining crew.

## Act V — The Right Question

### Scene 7 — Ko Estate · 7:20 P.M.

The family reviews the photographs. The grooves resemble marks on the Southern California artifact, though the match is not yet conclusive.

**AETHER GEAR:** Someone may have been working here before us.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** And we still don't know what they want.

**NATALICA:** Can I go into the mines tomorrow?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No.

**NATALICA:** With the farmer?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Still no.

**EPILOGUE — THE SEWERS · 10:16 P.M.**

Krobus examines a crack in the sewer wall. Silver light leaks through. Three heavy knocks echo beneath the town.

**KROBUS:** It's happening again.

Far below, a tiny red light appears in the darkness—and vanishes.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**NEXT: Episode 4 — The Shadow in the Sewers.**

# Episode 4 — The Shadow in the Sewers

**Monday, December 2, 2024**  
**Theme:** The machines aren't attacking the valley. They are searching beneath it.

## Cold Open — A Visitor at Midnight

### Scene 1 — Pelican Town Sewers · 2:17 A.M.

Krobus stands before a metal door that does not belong in the ancient drainage tunnels. Fresh drill holes surround the frame.

A red indicator flashes.

**MACHINE:** SUBSURFACE SURVEY. SECTOR FOUR.

**KROBUS:** Oh, no. Not again.

**MACHINE:** BIOLOGICAL PRESENCE DETECTED.

Krobus jumps back.

**KROBUS:** I live here!

**MACHINE:** NON-TARGET ORGANISM.

The light goes out.

**KROBUS:** How rude.

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — Monday at Ko Estate

### Scene 2 — Breakfast · 8:05 A.M.

Natalica studies the farmer's hand-drawn map. Carrie drinks cocoa. Aether Gear checks signal logs over breakfast.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Who's doing the dishes?

**NATALICA:** Carrie!

**CARRIE:** I did them yesterday!

**NATALICA:** Then you're experienced!

**AETHER GEAR:** Last person to finish washes up.

Natalica immediately stuffs the last piece of toast into her mouth.

**CARRIE:** Cheater.

Aether Gear announces the day's destination.

**AETHER GEAR:** We're visiting Krobus.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The person in the sewer?

**NATALICA:** He's not a person. He's a shadow person.

A long silence.

**AETHER GEAR:** He's a member of a local minority community.

**CARRIE:** Dad!

**AETHER GEAR:** Technically true.

### Scene 3 — The River · 10:20 A.M.

The farmer is fishing. After greeting the family, the farmer packs away a rod, a crab pot, a fish, and a recycling machine in the same backpack.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm not asking anymore.

**FARMER:** Ready?

**SAPPHIRE FOX (to Carrie):** Your father calls this person an ordinary farmer. Their daily workload would exhaust an engineering battalion.

**CARRIE:** They're very efficient.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** I'm starting to suspect they don't sleep.

**CARRIE:** Not after two in the morning, anyway.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** What was that?

**CARRIE:** Nothing.

## Act II — A Friend Underground

### Scene 4 — Sewer Entrance · 11:06 A.M.

The farmer produces a rusty key.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Where did you get a sewer key?

**FARMER:** Gunther gave it to me.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The museum curator?

**FARMER:** I donated a lot of things.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Public infrastructure works differently here.

### Scene 5 — Krobus's Shop

Krobus welcomes the farmer warmly, then shrinks back at the sight of the strangers. The farmer introduces them as friends investigating unusual machinery.

When Aether Gear opens the sample case, Krobus recoils.

**KROBUS:** Don't bring that near me!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why?

**KROBUS:** It wakes them up. The walking iron things. They've been digging below us.

**AETHER GEAR:** Robots?

**KROBUS:** They move without being alive. And they're not Dwarf machines.

He points down a narrow corridor.

**KROBUS:** They are digging for something.

## Act III — The Door Beneath the Town

### Scene 6 — Sealed Passage · 12:35 P.M.

Aether Gear studies the alloy door. Sapphire Fox kneels beside fresh tread marks.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Small tracked vehicle. Tight turning radius. Probably unmanned.

**AETHER GEAR:** A survey platform.

Carrie spots the same three-slash emblem on the frame.

**CARRIE:** That symbol looks familiar.

**AETHER GEAR:** Similar to the one on our California artifact.

**NATALICA:** So it's the same people?

**AETHER GEAR:** Related, perhaps. We can't prove that yet.

Sapphire Fox studies the excavation.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You're all asking who built these machines. I want to know what they're looking for.

A red light suddenly scans the group.

**MACHINE:** UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS DETECTED.

A half-height tracked survey robot rolls through the door. Its camera and manipulator arm swivel toward Aether Gear's sample case.

**MACHINE:** RECOVERABLE COMPONENT DETECTED. RETURN PROPERTY TO SURVEY SYSTEM.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Stop.

It continues forward. Sapphire Fox jams a metal bar into the tread. The farmer strikes the outer housing with a pickaxe. Aether Gear identifies and disconnects an exposed power connector.

**MACHINE:** MOBILITY ERROR.

The robot goes still.

**FARMER:** Think it drops loot?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** You're thinking about loot *now*?

## Act IV — A Search, Not an Invasion

### Scene 7 — Ko Estate Workshop · 4:50 P.M.

Aether Gear dismantles the recovered survey robot. Its controller has an unusual mixture of old and newer components.

**AETHER GEAR:** No manufacturer label. No ordinary commercial markings.

Natalica points at a board.

**NATALICA:** Is that an ESP32?

**AETHER GEAR:** No. Similar-looking package, though.

A plaque reads **SUBSURFACE RESOURCE SURVEY**.

**CARRIE:** So they're mining?

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Maybe. But a survey network is not the same as a mine.

She overlays the mine, sewer, and mountain anomaly locations on a map.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Someone built a system to investigate a specific region underground.

**AETHER GEAR:** And its control center should still be somewhere in the mountains.

**FARMER:** I know a route near there.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Of course you do.

### In-Game Quest Concept — Quest 03: The Hidden Survey

> **Objectives:** Inspect three suspicious mountain locations; photograph excavation marks; avoid machinery; report to Ko Estate.  
> **Reward:** 3,000g and increased Ko family friendship.  
> **Status:** Story concept; not implemented in v0.8.6-beta.

## Epilogue — The System Notices

### Scene 8 — Northern Mountains · 11:48 P.M.

An underground terminal flashes:

```text
SURVEY UNIT 04 — OFFLINE
UNAUTHORIZED HUMAN INTERFERENCE — CONFIRMED
EXTERNAL SIGNATURE DETECTED
MATCH FOUND: SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA INCIDENT
RECLASSIFY LOCAL ACTIVITY: PRIORITY HIGH
```

A larger machine wakes in the dark.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# Episode 5 — The Forgotten Machine

**Tuesday, December 3, 2024**  
**Theme:** An old machine may be doing a job no one remembers assigning it.

## Cold Open — Station 07

### Scene 1 — Underground Survey Station · 3:16 A.M.

A large machine starts inside an abandoned tunnel. An old terminal flickers green.

```text
AUTONOMOUS SURVEY NETWORK — STATION 07
SURVEY UNITS ONLINE: 3 / 8
COMMUNICATION LINK: DEGRADED
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: LOCATE SUBSURFACE ANOMALY
```

A tracked unit places a rock sample into a scanner.

```text
SAMPLE REJECTED
TARGET SIGNATURE NOT FOUND
CONTINUE SEARCH
```

Another alert appears.

```text
UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS: SECTOR 04
MISSING SURVEY COMPONENT
EXTERNAL INTERFERENCE: HUMAN
ACTIVATE SECURITY PROTOCOL
```

**CUT TO OPENING.**

## Act I — Old Technology, New Repairs

### Scene 2 — Ko Estate Workshop · 9:10 A.M.

Aether Gear examines the robot's circuit boards. Carrie records component numbers. Natalica studies the sensor assembly.

**NATALICA:** Is that LiDAR?

**AETHER GEAR:** Something similar. But the scan pattern is unusual.

**CARRIE:** Can we identify the manufacturer?

**AETHER GEAR:** Not yet. Some of this design is decades old. Other parts are much newer.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** So the machine has been repaired and upgraded over time.

She displays photographs of two different tread patterns.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** The smaller tracks belong to our survey robot. The larger ones suggest a heavier transport or construction platform.

**AETHER GEAR:** Then there's more equipment underground.

## Act II — Three Locations

### Scene 3 — Northern Mines · 10:25 A.M.

The farmer reaches the first survey point and photographs regular cuts in the rock. Behind a loose stone sits a powered sensor. Remembering Aether Gear's warning, the farmer leaves it in place.

**FARMER:** For once, I'll listen.

### Scene 4 — Old Mountain Trail · 12:40 P.M.

At the second point, the farmer finds a metal box marked with three diagonal lines.

```text
REMOTE NODE 12
STATUS: ACTIVE
```

The farmer photographs it and continues.

### Scene 5 — Abandoned Drainage Outlet · 2:15 P.M.

The third location conceals a dry concrete tunnel lined with power cables and fresh tread marks. A larger survey robot emerges from the darkness.

**MACHINE:** UNAUTHORIZED PERSONNEL DETECTED. LEAVE RESTRICTED AREA.

**FARMER:** I'm just taking pictures.

**MACHINE:** LEAVE RESTRICTED AREA.

The farmer thinks for a moment.

**FARMER:** Okay.

The farmer walks away. The robot, apparently satisfied, retreats.

## Act III — The Shape of the Network

### Scene 6 — Ko Estate · 4:30 P.M.

Aether Gear places the three locations on a map. They form a rough perimeter around an isolated section of mountain.

**AETHER GEAR:** These aren't random installations.

**Tentative setting:** Summer 2025, after the events of Phase I. The farmer invites the Ko family to visit Ginger Island after Willy's boat becomes available. As with Stardew Valley, Ginger Island is a **real place in the Combat Cinderella universe**, not a simulated game level or a portal destination.

**Premise:** The Ko family returns for what Sapphire Fox believes will finally be a relaxing tropical vacation. Carrie and Natalica, already familiar with the island from the game, are thrilled by the chance to explore. Aether Gear becomes fascinated with the volcano and the island's unusual engineering and natural phenomena. The farmer remains their local guide and the player's point of view. The island's mysteries should primarily grow out of **its own ecology, history, magic, ruins, and inhabitants**—not automatically connect back to Station 07 or the Southern California conspiracy.

The farmer remains the **player character**. The Ko family acts as quest-givers, companions in scripted scenes, and recurring NPCs. The main arc can be gated by introductory dialogue, friendship, mine access, and prior quest completion. Content Patcher can handle much of the dialogue, map, and event data; more elaborate persistent objectives and special interactions may require a SMAPI C# component.

# Appendix C — Continuity Guardrails

1. Stardew Valley is a real place within the Combat Cinderella universe. The family does not travel into a game or through a portal.
2. Carrie, Natalica, and Aether Gear know the game. Sapphire Fox does not.
3. The valley's magic, shadow people, monsters, and history exist independently of the Southern California mystery. Not everything is caused by ratmen or the same technology.
4. Station 07's connection to the wider mystery is suggestive, not a complete explanation of the main Season 1 conflict.
5. Sapphire Fox is a trained, capable operative, but also a recognizable parent with humor, blind spots, and imperfect everyday habits.
6. The farmer is a respected and indispensable protagonist, not merely an assistant to the Ko family.
7. The family returns to Southern California after the December 5 farewell. Their relationship with the valley continues.
