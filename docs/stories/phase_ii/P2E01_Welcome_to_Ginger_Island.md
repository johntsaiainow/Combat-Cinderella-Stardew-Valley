# P2E01 — Welcome to Ginger Island

**Phase II — The Ginger Island Adventure**  
**Status: Planned / story outline only; not yet written as a full screenplay.**  
**Tentative setting:** Summer 2025

## Planned Story

The farmer's invitation brings the Ko family to Willy's boat and then to the tropical island. Sapphire Fox declares this trip a vacation, while Carrie and Natalica are already planning excursions.

## Phase II — Opening Scene Concept (Draft, Not Final Script)

### Scene — Ko Residence, Southern California · Summer 2025

Natalica bursts into the living room, holding a letter from the farmer.

**NATALICA:** Dad! The farmer invited us to Ginger Island!

**AETHER GEAR:** Ginger Island? Willy got his boat running?

**CARRIE:** Wait, seriously? I want to see the volcano dungeon!

Sapphire Fox looks up from the sofa.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Hold on. Where exactly are we going this time?

**AETHER GEAR:** A tropical island.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Beaches?

**CARRIE:** Yes!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Somewhere to stay?

**NATALICA:** Yes!

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** No underground robots?

Aether Gear and the girls exchange a suspiciously long glance.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** Why did everybody stop talking?

**AETHER GEAR:** There *probably* aren't any.

**SAPPHIRE FOX:** That's what you said last time!

*End of preliminary Phase II opening concept.*
