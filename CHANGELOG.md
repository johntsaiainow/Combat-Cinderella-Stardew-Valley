# Changelog

Notable development milestones for **Combat Cinderella — Stardew Valley**.

This log describes the project's development builds. Earlier packages were prototypes or incremental patches, not necessarily standalone public releases.

## [0.8.6-beta] — 2026-10-25

### Added / Changed
- Expanded the Backwoods driveway terrain patch to five tiles across (X=33–37) with side verges and stylized rocky edges.
- Retained the northbound road (Y=0–12) and tested Backwoods → Ko Estate teleport at (35,1) and (36,1).
- Preserved Ko Estate return warp and existing NPC data.

### Known limitations
- Rocky cliff edges are approximations; the original Backwoods cliff geometry is not fully reconstructed.

## [0.8.5] — Development build

- Extended the driveway southward to Y=12.
- Revised the road texture and grassy transitions.
- Kept the northbound estate warp unchanged.

## [0.8.4] — Development build

- Extended the Backwoods driveway north to the map edge.
- Moved the estate warp to the northern end of the driveway.
- Verified northbound travel and teleport in-game.

## [0.8.3] — Development build

- Relocated the driveway center to Backwoods X=35, based on in-game coordinate testing.
- Reoriented the approach to run north.

## [0.8.2] — Development build
